Model governance
Approval readiness
Every material assumption needs a source, owner, confidence level, and review date.
01 · CONTROLLED
Reconciled outputs
  • Independent AI-cost inputs
  • Normalized pricing mix
  • Payment and refund formulas
  • Monthly customer and cash roll-forward
  • Break-even, CAC payback, simple LTV:CAC
02 · REQUIRED
Evidence before approval
  • Measured workload and provider invoices
  • Approved hiring and fully loaded payroll
  • Measured CAC, churn, refunds, support
  • Vendor quotes and load tests
  • Verified cash and financing schedule
03 · OUTSIDE MODEL
Finance work still required
  • GAAP revenue recognition and balance sheet
  • Debt, interest, depreciation, tax
  • Hiring dates by role and department
  • Annual contracts and geographic pricing
  • Pipeline probability and cohort retention
